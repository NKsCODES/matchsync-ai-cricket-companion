      Why this exists

      Nobody watches all 40 overs. Fans follow a match in snatches, between work, a cab ride and dinner. When they look back, the scorecard gives them the score but not the story.

      A fan coming back asks three things. What did I miss? Is it good or bad for my team? What do I watch now? This answers those three, in the fan's own voice, in about 20 seconds.

      Why it can be trusted

      A facts engine reads the ball by ball feed and ranks moments by how far they moved the win chance. The AI only picks the words. Every number it writes is checked against the fact sheet, and a line that fails is replaced by the engine's own line.

      Prototype by Niraj Koli. The match, teams and players are fictional. Win chance is a simple model estimate for storytelling, not odds.
